About the app

  • No account and no sign-in — just open the app and play.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no personal data collection.
  • The game works offline.

Support

Found a bug, or something in the game is not working as it should? Write to morning.musse@gmail.com and include your device model and Android version if you can — it makes problems much easier to find.

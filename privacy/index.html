Meow Mess Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Meow Mess is a cat-puzzle game. This policy explains what the app does with information — and, because the app is deliberately simple, most of the answer is “nothing”.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app collects no personal data.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • There is no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game works offline.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit or receive any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or device identifiers, and it has no feature that could send such information anywhere.

Data stored on your device

To remember how you left the game, the app stores a few values locally on your device:

  • your best score;
  • whether sound is on or off;
  • the interface language you picked (English or Ukrainian).

These values stay on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet and offline play

All game graphics, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app, so the game is fully playable without a network connection and makes no network requests while you play. The Android build declares the standard internet permission that the app framework includes by default; it is not used by the game.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs and no social or crash-reporting services. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google processes information about downloads and installations under its own privacy policy. That happens on Google's side, independently of the app, and we do not receive personal data from it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, because it does not collect information at all.

Your choices

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. To remove the locally stored values described above, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts using data in a different way, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
